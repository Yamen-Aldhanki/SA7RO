# SAHRO | سحرو

**Where Creativity Becomes Adventure**

SAHRO is a startup building interactive, game-based learning experiences for university students. We combine entertainment with real skill-building — turning academic challenges into engaging gameplay instead of passive studying.

## What we do

- Simulate real-world scenarios through gameplay
- Turn complex learning material into engaging, interactive experiences
- Help students build practical, career-ready skills through play
- Blend education and entertainment in meaningful, measurable ways

## Focus

- 100% Web-Based — no downloads, play anywhere
- GCC Region Focus — built for regional university students
- Infinite Learning — content that grows with our users

## Tech

Built with React, Tailwind CSS, and GSAP.
